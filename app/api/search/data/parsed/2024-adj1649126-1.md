# OPINION AND DECISION AFTER REMOVAL

# (En Banc)

Upon a unanimous vote of its members, the Appeals Board issues this decision as an en banc decision.¹ (Lab. Code, § 115.)

We previously granted removal in these matters on our own motion to provide an opportunity to study and address the issues. On October 21, 2024, and to assist the parties further, the Appeals Board issued an en banc notice of status conference, which occurred on November 4, 2024.

At the status conference, the parties entered into stipulations resolving their underlying attorney fee dispute in these matters and requesting dismissal with prejudice of all petitions for removal and/or disqualification in these matters as relates to their attorney fee dispute. We have reviewed the stipulations and as our Decision After Removal, we will issue orders approving the stipulation to withdraw the petitions, and order dismissal of all petitions for removal and/or disqualification pending in these matters.
