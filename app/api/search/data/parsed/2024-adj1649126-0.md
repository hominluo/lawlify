# NOTICE OF STATUS CONFERENCE

# (En Banc)

Upon a unanimous vote of its members, the Appeals Board issues this notice of status conference (En Banc). (Lab. Code, § 115.)

We previously granted removal in these matters on our own motion to provide an opportunity to study and address the issues. Thereafter, we issued our “Opinion and Orders (En Banc)” on September 4, 2024,¹ which consolidated these proceedings and ordered the parties to meet and confer and provide supplemental briefing. The parties timely filed responsive briefs, including an October 3, 2024 joint pleading wherein it appears that the parties have, in principle, stipulated as to how they wish to proceed.

To assist the parties further, we will issue an order setting this matter for a Status Conference with a designated hearing officer of the Appeals Board.

The parties should review their stipulations and be prepared to discuss the issues with the hearing officer.

¹ Commissioner Kelly was appointed to the Appeals Board on August 28, 2024, but did not participate in our previous order of September 4, 2024.
